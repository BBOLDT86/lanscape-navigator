The Prophecy Watch Will The Simpsons be right twice?

In March 2000, “Bart to the Future” put Lisa Simpson in the Oval Office with a budget crunch inherited from President Trump. The Trump half came true. This page tracks what Kalshi's traders pay for the other half, a woman next, and the name they attach to it, every day until Jan. 20, 2029.

The episode said a woman follows Trump. The market says: 10¢ on the dollar.

That is Kalshi's price that Alexandria Ocasio-Cortez is sworn in on Jan. 20, 2029, the woman the traders like most. Every woman on the board together comes to about 14¢. The favorite is a man.

How this page works. Once a day a script reads three of Kalshi's public markets (the 2028 winner, the Democratic nominee, and which party wins) and writes the prices here; the chart keeps one point a day. Prices are the last trade, or the middle of the bid and the ask when nothing has traded. A contract pays $1 if its person is inaugurated; the price is a crowd's bet, not a poll, and two years out the crowd is small. Nothing here is advice, and The Watch holds no positions.
