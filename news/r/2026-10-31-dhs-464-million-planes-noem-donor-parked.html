DHS paid $464 million for ten planes it could not fly. The last five were bought on Noem's final morning, at a markup.

A no-bid deal justified by urgency produced seven jets parked in Louisiana without crews, a private 737 nobody will take and a Gulfstream for the FBI director. The first deportation flights came this week, eleven months in, with senators' questions unanswered and a criminal inquiry circling the department's contracts.
