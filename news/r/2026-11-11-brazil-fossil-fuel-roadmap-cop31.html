Brazil hands the world a map out of fossil fuels. It binds no one, and Brazil is drilling more

At COP31 in Antalya, Brazil's COP30 team presented a voluntary roadmap that sorts countries by their dependence on coal, oil and gas and sets steps to 2033. Supporters call it a practical guide; skeptics point to Petrobras's record output.
