ICE paid $495.6 million for an empty Minnesota prison. The state had been offered it for $75 million.

CoreCivic's shuttered Appleton facility is assessed at $14.75 million. The sale closed the day it was signed, the company bought back $500 million of its own stock six days later, and the Treasury secretary, in Minnesota to announce a fraud crackdown, called the question a waste of time.
