Where this comes from. The places are OpenStreetMap's, read live from its Overpass service: names, kinds of food, addresses, websites and opening hours as people have mapped them. When a place hasn't listed its hours, the plan says so; check before you go. Weather and sunset: Open-Meteo. Place search: Open-Meteo and OpenStreetMap's Nominatim. The eight cities, their guides and what's on that weekend: Weekender. Map: CARTO and OpenStreetMap contributors.

Nothing you choose here is stored anywhere but this browser. The place you search for is sent to those services to look it up; with your own Anthropic key, the list of places is sent to Anthropic to write the plans.
