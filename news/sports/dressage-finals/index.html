Riders

    Where to sit

    • 1Behind C, sitting high. The judge's view of straightness and symmetry, and of a freestyle's floor plan as the rider drew it. Best for the Grand Prix freestyles.
    • 2Either long side, near E or B. Side-on to the extended gaits, half-passes, piaffe and passage. Best for the lower-level tests.

    Getting a good seat

    • Grandstand seating in WEC's main arenas is free and first come, first served.
    • The Sanctuary's dinner and awards break, 6:28 to 8:00 PM, is the time to claim seats for the Grand Prix Freestyle. The 6:30 awards ceremony is a show of its own.
    • Sit a few rows up: a freestyle's geometry reads better from above, and you see over the rail.
    • Take an aisle seat if you'll move between rings or leave right after the last ride.
    • Quiet during each test; save the applause for the final salute. No flash, phones on silent.

    Food & down time

    Each ring stops for lunch and dinner at its own time. Eat during the break of the ring you're watching and you won't miss a ride there.

    Where to eat on the grounds

    • International Grille & Café: breakfast, lunch and dinner with daily specials, beer and wine. Opens at 7 AM on show days, outside Roberts Arena near Vendor Village; the upstairs seating looks down on the Roberts ring.
    • Dark Horse Coffee Co.: Starbucks coffee, tea and pastries in Vendor Village, from 6:30 AM.
    • The Paddock Club is for exhibitors (from 4 PM on show days), and the Saturday Halloween party there is an exhibitors' event.
    • WEC doesn't post closing times for the café. If you're staying for the 8 PM class, eat during the 6:28 break rather than counting on a late meal.
    • Bringing your own: WEC's spectator FAQ doesn't address outside food. Ohio's open-container law covers sitting in or on a parked car in a lot open to the public, so keep any wine sealed in the car.

    Things to do between rides

    • The Finals trade fair: holiday shopping, horse-health booths and the official Finals merchandise. Vendors along the R+L walkway and in Vendor Village are open during show hours.
    • The awards in the Sanctuary at 11:47 AM, 6:30 PM and 10:00 PM. Every championship is pinned there.
    • Drop into a ring you didn't plan on: the Rings tab shows what's on now in all five.

    At the trade fair

      Getting there

      World Equestrian Center, 4095 OH-730, Wilmington, OH 45177

      • Parking is free. Park out front in the marked spots and walk in through the main gate; cars and trailers aren't allowed near the barns or rings.
      • Admission and grandstand seats are free. No tickets.
      • Well-behaved dogs on a leash are welcome at WEC's horse shows.
      • All five rings are indoors and climate-controlled. Allow about 10 minutes to walk between rings, more for Barn T.

      The drive home

      • Sunset is 6:34 PM and last light about 7:02, so any evening drive home is in the dark.
      • It's Halloween: expect trick-or-treaters and party traffic in towns along the way.
      • Late fall is peak deer season on the rural roads around the grounds. Watch the stretch between WEC and the interstate.
      • Clocks fall back at 2 AM Sunday, so you gain an hour overnight.
