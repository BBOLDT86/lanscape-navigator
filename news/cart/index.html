The numbers behind the chart

About the numbers

  • Shelf prices, in dollars. Each item is the average price people recorded in stores that month for one defined product (a dozen large eggs, a pound of ground beef), published by the Bureau of Labor Statistics as “average price data.” It is a different product from the CPI: no basket, no weights, no quality or substitution adjustments.
  • Your weights. The cart is fixed at what you picked, so if beef doubles you are charged double, not moved to chicken.
  • What it leaves out. Only items with a published price series: no rent, insurance, restaurants, or brand and store differences. An average is not your store; the register table above is the cross-check.
  • Gaps. No prices were collected in October 2025, so that month repeats September. An item with no series before your start date moves the start date, and the page says so.
  • The official lines are CPI “food at home” and CPI for all items, shown only for comparison.
