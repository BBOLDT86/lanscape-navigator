Every dollar as volume

Six makers of the Iran war's technology. Each glass cube holds as much volume as the company's Pentagon contract ceiling; the glowing cube inside holds the political money tied to the company or its leaders, on the same scale. How much of the glass the orange fills is the real ratio.

How to read it

Volume, not height: a cube twice as wide holds eight times as much. Anduril's $20 billion ceiling dwarfs its $3.2 million in political money, about 6,300 to 1. At OpenAI and Anthropic, with $200 million contracts, the money is a quarter and a fifth of the contract. Musk's own $366 million is the largest orange cube, set inside his companies' Pentagon business, at least $6.1 billion with SpaceX's launch award.

A ceiling is the most a contract allows, not money spent. Political money counts election giving this cycle by the company, its PAC and related people (Tech Influence Watch), gifts to the 2025 inauguration and the White House ballroom, Anthropic's two gifts to Public First Action and Musk's own spending through October 12.
