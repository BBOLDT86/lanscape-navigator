Getting found

  1. Add the site to Google Search Console (URL prefix https://bboldt86.github.io/lanscape-navigator/) and submit sitemap.xml. Do the same in Bing Webmaster Tools, which also feeds DuckDuckGo and ChatGPT search.
  2. Post one piece a day where its readers already are, with a tag: ?s=reddit, ?s=fb, ?s=text. The tags show up above.
  3. Every piece ends with a card that asks readers to pass it on; those links carry ?s=friend.

Counts by GoatCounter (no cookies), read back every two hours by news/pulse/fetch.py. A visit counts each thing once; a “read” needs five seconds on the page. This browser can be left out of the counts with the button at the top (or ?pulse=off on any page).
