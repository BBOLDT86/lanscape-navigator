How to read it

The Mandate for Leadership has 30 chapters in five sections, each written by people who went on to run the agencies they wrote about. The Watch tracks the most consequential proposals in each; a tower’s height is how many we track, and each block’s light is that proposal’s fate. The fly-through walks the five sections in order and pauses on the chapters that changed the most lives. The scorecard has every block as a row with its sources.

The Mandate is a section of The Watch, built on LANscape Navigator. Researched and written by Claude for The Watch; the record is kept in news/mandate/data/ and refreshed in weekly sprints. Built 2026-11-08T21:36-0500.

Nothing on this page is invented: every figure, quote, ruling and date carries a link to where it was read. Where sources disagree, the text says so. Corrections: open an issue on the repository.
