The vote-count claims, checked

What was claimed. In the week after the election, posts on X and Threads said Elon Musk’s Starlink satellite service had been used to alter tabulator counts. On Nov. 15, 2024, Stephen Spoonamore, a technologist who has long made election-security claims, published a “Duty to Warn” letter to Vice President Harris urging hand recounts in the swing states 50. The Election Truth Alliance, a Nevada nonprofit formed in early 2025, published an analysis of Clark County, Nevada, in June 2025 arguing that early-vote tabulators shifted after roughly 250 ballots toward about 60 percent Trump, that this “resembles” a “Russian tail” seen in falsified elections, and that the county’s early vote (Harris 40, Trump 59) diverged sharply from its mail (Harris 61, Trump 36) and election-day (Harris 47, Trump 50) votes 51 52. The group’s own disclaimer says “statistical anomalies, standing alone, are not proof of fraud, misconduct” 51.

What the officials found. The day after the election the director of the Cybersecurity and Infrastructure Security Agency, Jen Easterly, said “we have no evidence of any malicious activity that had a material impact on the security or integrity” of the vote 57. Election officials in Georgia and other swing states said Starlink played no part in tabulation; PolitiFact rated the claim false on Nov. 12, 2024, as did AP, Al Jazeera, Lead Stories and Check Your Fact 55 56. In Clark County, officials found about 1,600 uncounted mail ballots during the canvass, counted them, and reported no change in any result; all 17 Nevada counties certified 53 54. The Watch found no statement from Nevada’s secretary of state answering the Election Truth Alliance report specifically.

The president’s remark. At his Jan. 19, 2025 rally in Washington, Trump said Musk “knows those computers better than anybody. All those computers, those vote-counting computers, and we ended up winning Pennsylvania like in a landslide.” PolitiFact rated claims that this was an admission of rigging false: the remarks referred to Musk’s Pennsylvania campaigning, and the state’s certified systems keep paper records, are not connected to the internet and are publicly tested 58.

Pennsylvania and the model. The Election Truth Alliance’s Pennsylvania analysis leaned on a working paper by the University of Michigan political scientist Walter Mebane, whose “eforensics” model estimated potentially fraudulent votes exceeding Trump’s margin 59. Votebeat reported in September 2026 that the model was rejected by the American Political Science Review in 2020 and has not been peer-reviewed, that Mebane corrected data errors after Votebeat raised them and disputes the group’s characterization of his work, and that all ten election and statistics experts it consulted doubted the model can detect fraud; Stanford’s Justin Grimmer likened giving it to the group to “handing someone a stick of dynamite,” and the Hoover Institution’s Kirill Kalinin said “These are just anomalies, these are not election fraud.” Pennsylvania’s risk-limiting and spot-check audits affirmed the 2024 results 60. The group sued the Pennsylvania secretary of the commonwealth and three counties in November 2025 seeking a hand count (Election Truth Alliance v. Schmidt, W.D. Pa.) 61; a judge dismissed the case with leave to refile, and the complaint refiled in August 2026 no longer mentions Mebane 60 62.

Rockland County. SMART Legislation sued the Rockland County, N.Y., Board of Elections for a hand recount of the 2024 presidential and Senate results, citing voters who swore they had voted for Senate candidate Diane Sare in districts that recorded zero votes for her; the case survived early motions in 2025 63 64. On Dec. 16, 2025, Justice Rachel Tanguay dismissed it for lack of standing. Lulu Friesdat of SMART said, “We consider this a victory, even though we didn’t get a hand count”; the board said it would “continue to administer and certify election results in accordance with all applicable laws and procedures” 65.

Where it stands. No recount, audit or court has found that votes were altered in 2024; no court has reached the merits of the statistical claims, and the groups making them say they continue to seek hand counts.
