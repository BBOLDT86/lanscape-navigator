Laubacher holds 3,172-vote lead over Boebert in Colorado's most Republican seat

With about 97 percent of the expected vote counted, a retired Navy rear admiral leads the incumbent in the 4th District. Douglas County flipped in the statewide races, and every Eastern Plains county moved toward Democrats but stayed deeply Republican.

Four days after the polls closed, Colorado's most Republican congressional seat still has no winner. In the Associated Press count as of Saturday morning, Democrat Eileen Laubacher had 185,818 votes to 182,646 for Rep. Lauren Boebert, the Republican incumbent. That is a lead of 3,172 votes, or 0.8 points, with 96.65 percent of the expected vote counted. Douglas Mangeris, the Libertarian1 nominee (the AP lists him as an independent), had 12,598 votes, nearly four times the gap. The AP has not called the race.

The margin has been shrinking. The Denver Gazette reported that Laubacher led by more than 7,000 votes on election night and that her lead narrowed as Election Day ballots were added, which is typical in Colorado, where Republicans tend to vote later2.

The race

Laubacher is a former Republican from Highlands Ranch and a retired Navy rear admiral running for office for the first time3. She served as senior director for South Asia at the National Security Council under President Joe Biden4. She left the Republican Party for unaffiliated status in 2024 and registered as a Democrat in 20255. Boebert moved to Windsor and won the 4th in 2024 after two terms representing the 3rd District6; she moved across the state after nearly losing reelection in 20227.

According to the Denver Gazette, Laubacher raised nearly $14 million, more than 10 times Boebert's $1.2 million8. Outside groups spent $1.5 million against Boebert, mostly on ads about her "Beetlejuice" episode, and about $600,000 promoting her8.

Colorado Public Radio reported that Laubacher attacked Boebert's support for the Republicans' One Big Beautiful Bill, saying its Medicaid cuts would hurt rural hospitals, and blamed tariffs and the war with Iran for higher costs9. In their only debate, Boebert argued that state Medicaid cuts, not federal ones, were hurting rural hospitals and pointed to a $50 billion rural hospital fund in the law10.

Boebert also campaigned through a public break with President Donald Trump. She was one of four Republicans who signed the discharge petition forcing a vote on releasing the Epstein files11. Trump later vetoed her bill on the Arkansas Valley Conduit, a water pipeline for southeastern Colorado. He cited the project's cost, but some suspected he was angry over the Epstein files11. CPR reported that this spring he said he was open to a primary challenger and called her "weak-minded"9. In the campaign's final week, Trump endorsed her again, and Boebert told Colorado Politics the rift had been mended12.

The district

The 4th includes most of Douglas County and the Eastern Plains7, reaches into Loveland3 and takes in parts of Arapahoe, Larimer and Weld counties13. That matters for reading the wave map. Its moves come from the Senate and governor races and cover whole counties. Adams, Arapahoe, El Paso, Larimer and Weld are all split, with large parts in other districts. Those five moved 13 to 23 points toward Democrats, and they dominate the 15-point weighted move across the district's 21 counties.

Douglas County, the district's suburban core, voted R+7.0 for Trump in 2024 and D+4.1 this year, an 11-point move on 206,030 votes. Before the election, Colorado Politics reported that both parties put the county's shift down to newcomers, many from Denver14. In the House race, CBS Colorado reported that Laubacher led in Arapahoe, Douglas and Larimer while Boebert led in the Eastern Plains counties15.

The 15 smaller counties also all moved toward Democrats, and all of them stayed Republican by at least 30 points. Morgan County moved the most, more than 18 points, to R+30.1. Prowers moved 14 points and Phillips 13. Kiowa moved 2 and remains R+72.1.

A move is not persuasion. Weld cast 148,041 votes this year and Morgan 10,231, but county totals can't separate voters who changed their minds from Republicans who skipped a midterm or new residents who turned out.

What the result means

The last Democrat to represent the district was Betsy Markey, elected in 2008, who served one term7. The district then included Larimer County and Greeley16. If Laubacher's lead holds, Boebert would lose the seat she moved to after her 2022 scare.

Colorado orders an automatic recount when the margin is 0.5 percent or less of the winner's total17. On the current count that is about 929 votes, less than a third of the gap. If the AP's estimate of the total vote is right, roughly 13,000 ballots remain, and Boebert would need to win them by about 24 points to catch up. Her campaign said it is "investigating some very unusual voting patterns"2, and it expects a recount18 either way. Its lawyer, Scott Gessler, did not immediately respond when Colorado Politics asked which patterns he meant2.

This seat will not decide control of the House. The AP has already called 252 seats for Democrats, past the 218 needed. Statewide, Sen. John Hickenlooper won by 25.3 points and Phil Weiser won the governor's race by 27.7. Those are moves of 14 and 17 points from Kamala Harris's 2024 margin in Colorado.
