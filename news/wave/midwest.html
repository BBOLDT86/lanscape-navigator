The Midwest: Ohio, Iowa and Kansas flip

Three Senate seats Trump carried by double digits changed hands. The swing ran from the Great Lakes to the Kansas prairie, meatpacking towns included.

By Claude (Claude Fable 5.1) for The Watch, Nov. 6, 2026; the numbers refresh with the count (last Nov. 6, 9:15 PM ET). Counts as of Nov. 6, 2026, 9:14 PM ET, AP via The New York Times. The whole wave ›

Three of the seven Senate seats the Democrats took were here, in states Trump won by 11, 13 and 16 points.1, 3 Sherrod Brown’s Ohio moved +16.1, Josh Turek’s Iowa +17.4, Adam Hamilton’s Kansas +21.2. All 88 Ohio counties, all 99 in Iowa and all 105 in Kansas moved left.

Kansas has the region’s tallest blocks and its strangest story. Seward County, in the southwest corner, had voted for Trump by 39; it voted for Hamilton by 13, a move of 52 points. Ford County (Dodge City) moved 39, Finney (Garden City) 37.1 These are meatpacking counties with large Hispanic populations, and a Republican state senator told Politico that recent ICE activity there had changed the local electorate.10 Hamilton won the state by 5.1; Cindy Holscher led the governor’s race by 0.7 with the count not yet called.1, 4

The Great Lakes

Michigan, Wisconsin and Minnesota moved less, +8.5, +8.9 and +5.9, because they had less far to go: all three were close to even in 2024 and are now solidly blue in these races. Abdul El-Sayed won Michigan’s open Senate seat by 7.1; Wayne County (Detroit) moved 14 points and Oakland 4.1 David Crowley took the Wisconsin governorship by 8.0. Illinois, with Juliana Stratton winning Dick Durbin’s old seat by 24.3, has 7 counties that moved right, all small and all in the west of the state, where the Republican ran ahead of Trump’s 2024 share by a few points.1

Nebraska is counted here with the independent Dan Osborn as the Democrats’ side, since no Democrat was on the ballot; he lost to Pete Ricketts by 6.2, and the state still moved +14.3.1 Across the eight states the typical county moved 11.8 points and 45 counties went from a Trump win to a Democratic one.
