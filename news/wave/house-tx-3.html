Keith Self holds Texas' 3rd as Collin County swings toward Democrats

The McKinney Republican beat Air Force veteran Evan Hunt by 5.5 points in a redrawn district. Collin County moved 23 points toward Democrats in the statewide races, but the rural counties added to the east stayed deeply Republican.

Republican Rep. Keith Self held Texas' 3rd Congressional District, beating Democrat Evan Hunt 169,243 votes to 151,667. That is a margin of 5.5 points, or 17,576 votes, with 99% of the expected vote counted. The Associated Press called the race for Self at 2:09 a.m. Eastern on election night.

Self won on a night when Democrats James Talarico and Gina Hinojosa carried Texas for Senate and governor, by 12.1 and 7.1 points. Collin County, the suburban county at the center of his district, also voted Democratic at the top of the ticket. The seat stayed Republican because of the district lawmakers drew in 2025.

A campaign without a debate

Self is a former Collin County judge first elected in 20221 and an Army veteran2. Hunt, a retired Air Force lieutenant colonel from McKinney1, has not held public office and was unopposed in the March primary3.

The Greenville Herald-Banner reported that the two men differ on federal spending, the economy, national security and the challenges of North Texas growth4. Hunt made health care central. He told Ballotpedia that roughly ten percent of residents in the 3rd District rely on Affordable Care Act subsidies5.

The sharpest local fight was over a mosque. The Dallas Morning News reported that Self urged the council to reject the site plan to "stop the spread" of Shariah in Texas6. The McKinney City Council unanimously approved a site plan for a mosque for the McKinney Islamic Association after hours of raucous public comment7. Self then backed a McKinney recall drive targeting the mayor and three City Council members8.

Voters never saw the two men debate. On NewsNation, Self said he's not running against his opponent but against the general environment9. He rejected Evan Hunt's call for a debate. "This is my seat; I'm going to win it," Self said.9 The money was modest. According to the voter guide VoteSee, Keith Self has raised $887K and Evan Hunt has raised $703K, according to FEC reports through Oct 14, 202610, and VoteSee listed no public polls10.

A suburb tied to the countryside

The 2025 map was designed to flip five congressional seats from Democratic to Republican control11. On December 4, 2025, the U.S. Supreme Court ruled the 2025 map could be used for the 2026 elections.12 The new 3rd takes in Franklin, Hopkins and Delta counties, including Mount Vernon, Sulphur Springs and Cooper, part of Hunt County and most of Collin County, where the bulk of the district's voters live1. Morris and Titus counties, including Daingerfield, Lone Star, Naples, Omaha and Mount Pleasant, have moved to the 3rd District1 from the 1st.

On The Watch's wave map, Collin County went from R+11.2 for Donald Trump in 2024 to D+12.1 in this year's statewide races. That is a 23-point move on 457,413 votes. Hunt County, around Greenville, moved 22 points but still voted R+33.9. Titus County moved 30 points, the largest shift in the district and more than the state's 25.8-point Senate move, yet it stayed at R+24.8. Hopkins moved 19 points. Delta, Franklin and Morris moved 14 to 15 points and stayed between R+37.5 and R+55.2. Weighted by votes, the district's counties moved 22.9 points, a little less than the state as a whole.

KERA reported that in Collin County, Hunt pulled past Self2. If Hunt led there and Self won the district, the arithmetic says Self's margin came from his part of Hunt County and the five East Texas counties. Those five cast 32,460 votes in the statewide races, and each still voted Republican by about 25 points or more.

These moves show how places voted, not why. A county count cannot separate voters who switched parties from changes in who turned out. Collin's 457,413 statewide votes include both. KERA noted that voter turnout in 2026 — like in 2022 — still trailed behind the 2018 elections13.

What the result means

The GOP has held the seat since a 1968 special election.14 Self was re-elected15 easily in 2024 under the old lines. NBC DFW noted that Self and fellow Republican Pat Fallon both won reelection, although Self's race was significantly closer16. Self's district sits in a changing region. NBC DFW reported that Collin County delivered major Democratic wins and flipped seats16, and The Dallas Morning News reported that Democrats swept statewide races and won control of the Texas House17.

Self returns to a House where AP has called 252 seats for Democrats, more than the 218 needed for a majority, with 22 still uncalled. In the 3rd, about 1% of the expected vote remains, and local papers caution that results are not official until canvassed18.
