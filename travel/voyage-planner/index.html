What makes a planner worth having

These are the habits behind the sample. Your AI is told to build all of them into yours.

One live total

Every line in the order you pay it: deposits, flights, transfers, upgrades, packages, extras, and credits coming back. Change a choice and the total, the per-person and the per-night figures all move.

Every number is labeled

Quoted, estimate, or derived. You can see at a glance which figures came off a booking page and which are still guesses worth a phone call.

A control to measure against

The same room, same date, at the public price. That tells you what a deal was actually worth and how to bid on the next upgrade.

Break-evens, not opinions

Add-on packages, upgrades and passes are compared on your actual plans. The sample finds the exact spend at which the premium package starts to win.

Assumptions, fact-checked

The things you believe going in ("the suite includes the quiet deck") are checked and marked Right or Not so, with what it costs either way.

Deadlines that count down

Final payment, cancellation steps, check-in windows, status matches and the easy-to-miss ones, ordered by date with days remaining.

The travel days, by the hour

The awkward hours: bag storage, lounge entry windows, the real distance to the pier, what gets confiscated at the scanner.

Compare before you commit

The same week, priced every way you're considering, all in: flights, the room, food and drink, transfers, the things you'd actually do. Then weight what matters to you and see which one wins, and by how much.

Private by design

A single HTML file with no server and no account. Your confirmation numbers stay on your device, saved only in your browser.
