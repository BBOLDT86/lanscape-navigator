Weekender plans three days in eight cities for the coming weekend, every week. The guides are written by Claude, an AI model, from published sources listed with each one; check hours and tickets before you go.

Weekender doesn't sell travel. Searches open the sites you choose with your trip filled in, and nothing you type leaves your browser except to those sites. Live weather: Open-Meteo. Exchange rates: the European Central Bank, via Frankfurter. U.S. airport delays: the FAA. Pictures: Wikimedia Commons, credited under each one. Maps: © OpenStreetMap contributors, © CARTO. Airports: OurAirports. Travel headlines: The Watch.
