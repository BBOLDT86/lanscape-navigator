How the numbers work

Stall income

Revenue stalls = existing stalls + planned stalls − stalls you keep for yourself. Monthly board = revenue stalls × board rate × occupancy, less the operating-cost share if you set one. At $500 and 70%, each stall earns $4,200 a year before hay, bedding and labor.

Where a listing doesn't give a stall count I've entered my best read of the barn and marked it "assumed." Fix it in Details.

Arena and barn costs

With "Any arena," a property with no arena gets an outdoor sand arena added. With "Indoor required," anything without an indoor gets a 60×120 indoor added. HomeAdvisor's June 2026 guide puts a 60×120 indoor at $150k–$250k; I default to $225k for footing, lighting and kickboards. The outdoor figure and per-stall barn costs are my estimates.

Valuation

"My value" is a contributory read: house by size, age and finish against nearby sales; barn and arena at depreciated replacement cost; land at local per-acre levels. It's checked against the comps above. Zillow's bot check blocked the browser after one property, so Kilbourne Rd is the only listing with a real Zestimate. On the Zestimate setting, every other listing falls back to my value and says so.

Loan and income

Loan = price basis × (1 − down), plus improvements if you roll them in. PITI = principal and interest, the listed tax bill (or my estimate where marked), and insurance on the all-in cost. Income needed = PITI × 12 ÷ the housing ratio you set (20% default). Lenders rarely credit boarding income without two years of returns, so that toggle is off by default.
