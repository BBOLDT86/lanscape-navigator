    What you're looking at

    Section 106 is on the long side of the floor, one section closer to the stage than 105 — second section from the stage end. Row G is about seven rows up from the floor rail, so you're close to floor energy with a clean, slightly elevated line to the booth and the production. Seated, the stage is to your left; the mix position and back of the floor are to your right.

    You'll enter 106 from the main concourse and walk down. Rows at the bottom of the section (A–F) will have people standing the whole set; plan on standing too, this is a DJ show.

    Venue logistics

    No bags. At all. Strict

    The Schott has a no-bag policy: bags and purses of any size are turned away. Only a wallet or wristlet up to 5" × 8" × 1" gets in. There is no bag check, so anything you bring you carry on you, pockets or wristlet only.

    Tickets

    Mobile SafeTix only. Add both tickets to Apple Wallet before you leave; screenshots and printouts are rejected, and arena signal is bad at the doors.

    106 G seats 20–21, bought together as one order. Make sure both show in the Ticketmaster app and both are in Apple Wallet before you leave.

    Doors & entry

    Doors typically open 60–90 minutes before showtime at this building, so expect 5:30–6:00 PM. Any entrance works for reserved seats; follow signs for 100-level sections on the main concourse.

    Show is 18+ recommended, all ages admitted. Bring ID for drinks.

    Money

    The building is fully cashless: card or phone pay for concessions and merch. No re-entry once you're in.

    Coats & weather

    No coat check. Mid-November, a 7-minute walk to the pickup lot at 11:20 PM: wear a jacket you don't mind sitting on, or layer so you can shed in the bowl. It gets hot in there.

    Mikey's Late Night Slice

    Workable. Here's how.

    Every Mikey's is open past midnight on Fridays, so the tradition survives. Your Uber is a 10-minute ride home, which means the pizza stop is a short detour rather than a second trip.

    Option A, add a stop: in the Uber app, edit the reservation and add Mikey's as the first stop with home as the final destination (or have the driver add it at pickup). Stops are meant to be a few minutes, so order ahead online for pickup around 11:35 and it's grab-and-go.

    Option B, deliver it: place a delivery order from the arena at ~11:00 timed for midnight at home. Zero detour, slightly less fun.
