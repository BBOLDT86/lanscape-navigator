His first arena tour, and it’s a full show.

John Summit used to be an accountant. CTRL ESCAPE is him quitting, in front of 18,000 people, every night for two hours.

The tour trailer.

Here’s what people coming out of the Boston show described. There’s a glass office suspended above the stage, with desks, old computers, and dancers in suits working through the set. Lasers and fire columns build a cage of light around the booth. A “performance review” kiss-cam lands on the crowd and ends with people stamped hired or fired. Partway through he leaves the stage and plays from a platform in the seats.

Musically it’s house: big vocals, long builds, drops you feel in your chest. He opens with the new album, pulls in remixes of songs you know (Gaga, Gwen Stefani, Lana), and closes with “Don’t Believe It.” The suit comes off piece by piece. He finishes in a tank top.
