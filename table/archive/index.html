  • 12:33Granita into the freezer (scraped at 1:10, 1:45, 2:20, 2:55, 3:30; fluffed at 4:05).
  • 1:12Apple pie in (425°F 20 min → 375°F 45 min). Stock strained; the old stuffing assembled per the card, no eggs, sourdough.
  • 1:17Gravy base and cranberry sauce made while the pie baked. Lunch.
  • 1:45Six highballs into the freezer with a rosemary sprig against the wall, misted at every granita scrape until glazed.
  • 2:17Pie out. Walnut cream and cranberry-chili jam done.

  • 3:10Vinaigrette; hazelnuts, almonds, pepitas toasted and labeled by course; beans blanched in the 12" skillet; no cashews → lemon walnut cream for the soup garnish.
  • 3:48Front-loaded the evening: potatoes cooked and puréed, brown butter and sage fried, coconut cream whipped, pie cut, pecorino shaved — all before the oven went on.
  • 4:49Bird in, 425°F, uncovered. Showers.
  • 6:05Not done — no thermometer, so the leg-wiggle and thigh-juice tests.
  • 6:30Doors. Spritzes. Gougères passed.
  • 6:39Bird out (110 min). Stuffing in at 350°F, covered 30 / uncovered 20. Gravy finished in the roasting pan with prosecco.

For next time

read this first
  • Buy an instant-read thermometer. The hand tests worked, but 6:05–6:39 was the only tense half hour of the day.
  • A flat 13-lb bird at 425°F took 110 minutes, not 90. Plan 1h50 from a 4:45 start to be out by 6:35 with guests at 6:30 — or put it in at 4:30.
  • Front-loading was the whole trick. Purée, brown butter and sage, whipped coconut cream, pie cut, nuts, beans, vinaigrette — all done by 4:40. The evening was one five-minute bird-out, one three-minute toss, and one fifteen-minute burst. Do this again, earlier.
  • Make the day before: pie dough, gravy base, cranberry sauce and jam, walnut cream, rosemary highballs in the freezer, spritz base. Saturday morning should be squash, bird, stock, pie, gougères.
  • Granita needs 3½ hours from pour to fluff in the 7×10 ceramic; scrape every 35 minutes. Start it by noon.
  • Order check: cashews and white cranberry juice weren't on the Whole Foods list; pepitas were and got forgotten. Lemon walnut cream (3 tbsp walnut cream + 1 tsp lemon + salt) is actually the better soup garnish — keep it.
  • Pots: choux in the 9" cast iron and beans blanched in the 12" skillet both worked fine. The velouté pot is free once the soup is in the fridge.
  • The two-screen command center (laptop in the kitchen on Today, phone for reading) with the "Right now" card and the at-a-glance strip was the right interface. Checkboxes are per-device; that was fine.
  • Menu cards: black-and-white on the laser printer looked right; cream card stock next time. Two tall cards per letter sheet, cut down the dashed line.
  • The NFC guest page with the iMessage/Android preview card was a hit — the link is reusable; change the title and courses.
  • Coupe White low bowls from World Market Lennox (SKU 659324, $7.99) are the soup bowls now. Buy a sixth.
  • Seat at 7:00 sharp and the seven courses land coats at 10:00. Protect the seat time; the rest follows.
