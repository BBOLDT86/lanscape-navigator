The bird: one 13-lb turkey, two ways

No breast, no duck — so the whole bird does both jobs. Spatchcocked, it roasts in about 90 minutes at 425°F with the thighs done when the breast is, and the oven is free again by 6:45 for the stuffing.

8:00 am — spatchcock and salt

  • Bird breast-side down. Kitchen shears along both sides of the backbone; pull it out. Flip, press hard on the breastbone until it cracks and the bird lies flat. Tuck the wing tips behind the breast
  • Pat very dry. 3 tbsp Diamond kosher (2 tbsp Morton), 2 tsp sugar, 1 tsp pepper: fingers under the skin of the breast and thighs, the rest over the skin
  • On a rack over a rimmed sheet pan, fridge, uncovered, until 3:45. If it was salted last night, skip the salt — just flatten it
  • Backbone, neck, giblets (no liver) → the stockpot with an onion, carrot, celery, bay, peppercorns, water to cover; bare simmer to noon; strain; this replaces a quart of the bought broth in the gravy base

3:45 — temper; 5:00 — in

  • 3:45out of the fridge; rough-cut onion, carrot, celery and the apple cores under the rack to catch drippings
  • 4:40oven to 425°F
  • 5:00bird in, skin brushed with 2 tbsp melted butter. Rotate the pan at 5:45
  • 6:15first check: 150°F in the thickest breast, 170°F in the thigh. Usually 6:30–6:45 for 13 lb flat
  • 6:45out; tented loosely; rests on the board until 7:50. Oven to 350°F — stuffing goes in

7:50 — carve two ways

  • Legs off at the joint. Thighs boned (the bone pulls free after resting), each cut into three skin-on blocks → six; five plated, one for the cook
  • Cast iron hot with 1 tbsp butter: thigh blocks skin-side down 2 min until the skin shatters. This is the "second way"
  • Breast lobes off the bone whole, sliced across the grain ⅜ inch — two or three slices a plate
  • Drumsticks and wings go back to the kitchen for Sunday. Carcass into the stockpot with tonight's dishes

If the fridge can't take a flat 13-lb bird

  • Legs off now (thigh + drumstick, one cut through the hip joint) — the crown and the legs each fit a half-sheet pan, stacked on the cleared shelf
  • Roast them together at 5:00 on two racks; the legs come out when the thighs hit 170°F, same window
  • Everything else above holds

Flat, it roasts in about 90 minutes at 425°F and the thighs finish with the breast. The thigh blocks get a 2-minute skin-side sear in cast iron at 7:35 — that's the "second way." The backbone and neck make real turkey stock for the gravy this morning. Menu line stays "turkey two ways."

If something slips

  • pie runs lateit only has to be out before the gougères go in — push gougères to 2:30, nothing else moves. Worst case the pie bakes while you shower at 4:00
  • Viv back latethe table gets set at 4:00 while you shower; the bowls just need a rinse
  • bird slowit has until 7:00 to hit temperature — the stuffing just goes in later and holds. Mains at 8:20 is fine
  • no room in the fridge for a flat birdlegs off; crown and legs on two half-sheets, stacked. Same roast, same window
  • granita froze solidlet it sit out 10 min, scrape hard with a fork — it comes back. Or pulse it in the blender for 2 seconds
  • sprig won't stick to the glassit doesn't have to — a frozen sprig standing in a frosted glass reads the same. Pour over it
  • prosecco not cold by 6:3020 minutes in the ice bucket with a handful of salt in the ice
  • breast slowthe salad buys 15 minutes. Mains at 8:20 is fine
