Where things are in the store

World Market stores share one floor plan; this is that plan, not a survey of this building. There's a department map by the door and any associate will point you — the whole trip is three stops.

  1. Dining, center of the store. The dinnerware wall is the long run of open-stock plates and bowls. White porcelain is grouped together; the Coupe collection has its own shelf tag. Bowls are usually one shelf below the plates of the same line.
  2. Table linens, right next to dining. Napkins are folded on a table or in cubbies by color; the washed-linen ones are the softest, slightly wrinkled ones. Count five of the same color.
  3. Candles, on the decor wall. Tapers hang in two-packs; holders are on the shelf beside them. The unscented votives are in the same bay, usually low.
  4. Pickup order: the registers at the front — tell them it's a store pickup, show the confirmation email or the order number on your phone, and bring ID.

Members get 10% off store-pickup orders with code STORE10; joining World Market Rewards is free at the register or in the app, and it applies to the bowls if you place the order before you walk in.

Back home

  1. Bowls through the dishwasher or a hot rinse — they're the soup bowls, and they get warmed under the hot tap at 6:10 anyway.
  2. Napkins out of the packaging, pressed if there's an iron handy, folded once lengthwise for the menu card to tuck into.
  3. Tapers into the holders down the center of the table now, unlit. Votives at both ends.
